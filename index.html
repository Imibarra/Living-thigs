How to play

Choose a challenge and answer questions about Living Things. You can play without a timer.

  1. 1Choose your game: 12, 24 or all the questions. Each round includes different types of activities.
  2. 2Answer in different ways: A/B/C, true or false, choose all, complete, match, order and classify.
  3. 3Check your answer: see what is correct and read a short explanation.
  4. 4Keep going: earn points, follow your progress and review your mistakes at the end.
